Zolva

Puzzles, Reimagined — Sudoku for Android

Zolva is a clean, offline-first Sudoku game. Play across four difficulties, take on a new Daily Challenge each day, track your best times, and race friends head-to-head when you feel like going online.

Features

  • Sudoku from easy to expert
  • A fresh Daily Challenge every day, resumable any time
  • Fully playable offline — no account needed
  • Per-difficulty saves, so nothing is ever lost
  • Statistics, best times, and a hint wallet
  • Optional leaderboards, friends, and live races

Privacy

Zolva asks for no personal information to play. Read the full Privacy Policy.
